# Mathematical Modeling of Motor Vehicle Crashes in Massachusetts

## Math Modeling Competition — 2025–2026

This repository contains our 25-page mathematical modeling research paper investigating the rate of motor vehicle crashes in the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.

### Project Overview

We developed a mathematical/statistical model to investigate factors associated with motor vehicle crashes and proposed potential solutions for reducing crash rates.

The project incorporated data analysis and a Python-based logistic regression model to analyze crash-related factors and evaluate potential interventions.
